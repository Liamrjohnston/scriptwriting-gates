# Complete gate register

Read with [workflow](workflow.md) and the entire [Humanizer](humanizer.md). Use these stable IDs; do not renumber or introduce G31. G18a/b are inside G18; D1–D7 inside G26; the 33 Humanizer checks inside G25; H1–H4 are separate hook checks. Assess G26 and G30 last even when displaying numerical order.

Each result needs its actual basis: exact spoken quotation, source and checked date, real artifact, reference transcript/metric, or explicit editorial reasoning. A sentence's presence proves only presence, not persuasion. Distinguish mechanical checks, resolving receipts, editorial judgments and independent assessments. Do not combine two gate verdicts into one.

## G1–G30

| ID | Check | Required decision and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | Send trigger | Name sender, recipient, real relationship, immediate shared situation and what forwarding says about the sender. Roles suffice; do not need private names. For consumer topics a buyer/partner or training-buddy relationship is valid. A generic topic fan is not a trigger. If the intended format genuinely has no share action, declare that before drafting and explain N/A here and G23; do not pretend an absent trigger passed. |
| G2 | Topic territory | Name the actual account's content territory and why this topic belongs. Use the audience's goal, problem, capability, money or status stakes. There is no AI/dev/repository-only restriction and no requirement to invent a money angle for an educational fitness topic. An attractive feature is not yet a meaningful concept. |
| G3 | Consequence-led opening | Quote the concrete payoff, money consequence, recognized relevant authority, threat or status consequence before an unfamiliar product/name consumes attention. A bare unfamiliar name or feature announcement fails. Emotion must be grounded in actual stakes, not shame or fabricated danger. |
| G4 | Hook length and timing | Quote/count the exact opening; default roughly 10–18 words and 3–6 seconds, clarity before rigid timing. Record measured human or synthetic rehearsal separately from estimated duration. An explicit different format/voice can change the target; word count never proves spoken seconds. |
| G5 | Opening-line tell scan | Scan the actual first sentence for fake-candid pauses, stock announcement/openers, unexplained pronouns, vague placeholders and formulaic reversals. Default reject “Here's the thing”, “Honestly?”, “Look”, “Real talk”, “It's not X, it's Y”. “Most” or “This” is not universally banned based on another account's private results: require the sentence to establish its referent and consequence, and use this account's evidence if available. |
| G6 | Every-sentence escalation | Print one row per sentence: exact text, new value/heat, viewer reaction and improvement over the preceding sentence. Check pronoun antecedents and seams. Repeated growing fear or a distinct useful task roster can climb on one axis. No rotation quota, compulsory threat or automatic anaphora ban. Equal/descending interest, smaller feature decomposition, empty connection, boilerplate reassurance or a gratuitous billing detour is REVISE. Labels do not rescue flat copy. |
| G7 | Saveable artifact exists | Name/inspect the actual reusable checklist, guide, worksheet, comparison, board, routine or other useful artifact. It must be worth keeping independently of commenting. A real asset can pass before filming; BLOCKED means it does not exist or cannot be verified, not merely that footage is missing. Specify a readable screenshot/hold plan in production notes. A URL string alone does not prove availability. |
| G8 | Viewer-first point of view | Scan full spoken text for first-person words and variants. Default zero; an explicit first-person brand voice, testimony or CTA choice can customize it. Record the current user's actual direction, not a reviewer-invented exception. Even approved first person must serve the viewer and cannot invent a personal experience. |
| G9 | Audience-native relevance | Quote a recognizable shared task, burden or consequence. Specific industry examples are optional, not sentence quotas. For a broad audience, put the shared pain first; a later narrow demonstration must not become an unnecessary entry requirement. |
| G10 | Plain English | Run the audience-configured jargon scan and then review every unfamiliar term editorially. Translate into the audience's real object or consequence; keep the counted noun with every number. A car buyer may understand “range” but not a battery acronym; an expert audience can keep terms that a beginner cannot. No global inherited ban on unrelated words. Necessary product names must not imply unsupported compatibility or scope. |
| G11 | Spoken list discipline | Default no three-item recital inside one spoken sentence: repeated determiner lists and plain “A, B and C” lists fail. Collapse the whole verified bundle into an accurate collective term; do not drop an item or replace the triple with a pair just to clear the scan. A growing roster across distinct escalating sentences is not the same pattern. Check replacement for broader implied coverage. Appositives and connected clauses are not automatically lists. |
| G12 | Specific useful CTA resource | Quote the resource promise and identify/verify the exact guide, report, routine, comparison or other useful thing. Generic “follow for more” is not a resource. If the explicitly selected campaign goal is booking/purchase rather than a resource, record the purposeful variation and assess the specific real next step; do not silently swap the user's CTA for comments. Distribution/automation test is separate from existence. |
| G13 | No spoken URLs or long prompts | Scan spoken copy for literal URLs, handles and long verbatim instructions/prompts; keep actual strings on screen/in the resource. The visual notes can contain them. |
| G14 | No stage directions in narration | Scan for brackets, camera cues, editing instructions and document markup inside the spoken section. Move them to separate production notes. |
| G15 | Accuracy and source traceability | Every factual claim, number, name, deadline, price, capability and attribution maps to dated evidence. Distinguish primary documentation, user-supplied assertion, observed test, inference and historical metric. A brief is a requirement/lead, not independent substantiation. No invented personal test, eligibility, revenue, savings, urgency or outcome guarantee. |
| G16 | Concrete stakes | Quote the viewer's recognizable recurring burden, opportunity or consequence early, before an unfamiliar product name. A real clock may strengthen stakes but no mandatory deadline sentence. Desire, fear and relief can arise through the actual task. Do not manufacture insecurity or danger. |
| G17 | Loaded question | State the specific question the early lines let a viewer ask while understanding the premise. Load it early, approximately the first eight seconds in a short reel. Quote the setup as evidence, label the question itself an inference. Empty “it gets crazier”/“the real unlock” teasers fail. Timing stays estimated unless measured. |
| G18 | Upward reveal, plan and execution | G18a: expected prediction and larger true reveal declared before drafting. G18b: actual spoken reveal and prior clue. A limitation is not an upward twist; random surprise is confusing. Where no supported upward reveal exists, explain N/A and preserve steady escalation rather than manufacture one. |
| G19 | Re-hook seams | Quote each major transition; it adds new fact, implication, consequence or a genuine question. A connector alone fails. A roster can continue naturally without a compulsory close-one/open-another formula. |
| G20 | Account-level fit and breadth | Mark CORE/ADJACENT/PERIPHERAL relative to the specified audience, not a borrowed account. Name entry prerequisites and shared recurring burden. Separate broad growth content from useful bounded sponsor/specialist work. Inspect opening pair for avoidable exclusions. Five maturity states inside one niche do not prove broad audience fit. Actual product scope cannot be widened by wording. |
| G21 | Honest measured-reference transfer | Inspect 5–10 candidates where available; disclose a smaller real cohort. Name primary/support parents, actual transferred device, transcript, identifier/link and comparable dated performance observations. For share-oriented short-form, prefer age-comparable shares/views over lifetime view totals; other goals need a stated relevant metric/window. Approved examples alone are not winners. Complete closest analogue, weak/repeated control, recent coverage and transfer decision below. Missing measured history stays BLOCKED with a useful provisional draft, never invented or N/A. |
| G22 | Exact demo is real and filmable | Verify actual starting state → exact action → readable result → viewer consequence. The product, run/result or authentic explanatory artifact must exist and be filmable now. A proposed shot list is insufficient. Footage need not exist yet. Label fixture, illustration, rendering, documentation, observed test and real account scope; an illustration cannot substantiate a claimed real performance result. Align with D7 causal explanation. |
| G23 | Recipient-specific share line | Quote the final share invitation and match it to G1's actual recipient/pain. “Someone who needs this” fails. A share line cannot rescue a concept without a real trigger. Explicit non-share formats may declare N/A consistently with G1, never pretend to meet the default. |
| G24 | Maximum defensible promise | Preserve four truth-sheet buckets. Use strongest supported outcome/capacity, not safest generic phrasing and not a fabricated result. Full capability survey explains selections/combinations/omissions. A small sample need not cap documented capacity; it cannot prove unsourced large-scale results. Keep spoken promise accurately scoped and verified secondary pricing/setup detail in resource. If an omitted condition changes the promise materially, narrow it or include the qualification: no misleading “free”, guaranteed success or hidden required payment. Keep required sponsorship disclosure. |
| G25 | Entire Humanizer, parity and read | Read the complete included reference and individually audit all 33 patterns. Record exact evidence or justified N/A, final factual and locked-wording parity, and rehearsal method. A punctuation scan is only a subset. Never invent a human listening test, actual recording or measured duration. Restore real voice if cleaning produces sterile prose; rerun review. |
| G26 | Intended viewer's desire and clarity | Run last with G30. Assess recognizable routine/pain, term translation, practical consequence, first-listen clarity, each sentence and the full ladder from this viewer's seat. Assess all D1–D7 below. Answer applicable task/outcome, delegated work, compounding effort/cost, verified contexts and friction questions; irrelevant developer/token questions become N/A, not fabricated fitness/car facts. Meaning and desire require editorial judgment, not regex approval. |
| G27 | Researched authority and attribution | For each authority appeal record claim → real identity/owner → dated primary source → relevant accomplishment → wording/scope → result. Distinguish third-party work from official release/endorsement. Famous-but-irrelevant names, invented credentials and prestige by association fail. Missing evidence BLOCKED; contradicted/exaggerated attribution REVISE; no authority appeal N/A. H4 can instead be earned by directly observable proof. |
| G28 | Evidence-backed hook device | Name actual opening shape and measured parent with transcript/ID and comparable evidence. Viewer-led, crowd contrast, enviable-result contrast and recognized-entity openings are candidate shapes, not universal winning laws. Never copy private performance claims across industries. Unidentified pronouns, unplaceable names and incomprehensible premises fail. Cold start remains BLOCKED, even if the hook sounds strong. |
| G29 | Five audience states | For this topic map never done it, just started, paying someone, struggling alone, competent/seeking an edge. Default hook: four STAY, first two mandatory; map body benefit for every applicable state. One real outcome can serve several, no dedicated line quotas. Genuine topic/brief-specific N/A must not hide exclusion or a missing beginner payoff; intentional specialist targeting is disclosed. This maturity test does not replace G20 breadth. |
| G30 | Promise-raised doubts and understandable units | Identify material doubts created by this actual promise; where unresolved, quote real capability/proof that removes doubt while increasing value. Answer inline or in an earned next beat, not a compulsory Q&A. No minimum objection count, automatic same-tools/login ending, one-change ritual, fixed late placement or irrelevant cost disclaimer. If no doubt remains, explain N/A for that subcheck. Translate numbers into audience-felt units with correct sourced arithmetic; absent quantities N/A. Verify control and setup claims. |

## D1–D7: desire and contrast inside G26

Quote actual spoken setup/payoff for each applicable check. The desired conclusion and mechanism interpretation can be reasoned from those lines; do not pretend they are verbatim audience testimony. For a pure text-reference post, use its actual visible organizing logic instead of inventing narration. These are persuasion/editorial tests, not claims about neuroscience or guaranteed conversion.

| ID | Question and decision |
|---|---|
| D1 | Recognizable burden: what actual recurring task, dependency, uncertainty or goal does this viewer recognize before the offer? No manufactured insecurity. |
| D2 | Meaningful before/after: what substantial useful change does the intervention provide over the stated current workflow? Name real task/service/capability and burden changed; no pretend replacement of a department or unsupported competitor inferiority. |
| D3 | Viewer’s new role: what do they stop handling blindly, and what can they decide, review or do instead? A buyer becomes a comparator, an exerciser gains a usable decision, an owner reviews work. Real control can answer doubt inline; no compulsory role-flip sentence. |
| D4 | Genuine cost of staying put: what actual repeated effort, money, correction, uncertainty or missed decision remains? Quantify only with evidence; no fake deadlines, invented bills or “everyone else is ahead”. |
| D5 | Credible contrast: which source and real filmable artifact demonstrate the change? Documentation/proposed demo is not a completed performance test. An existing truthful sample can support script stage; finished footage is a later check. |
| D6 | Earned viewer conclusion: state what they can infer and quote the setup/payoff earning it. Remove begging, insults, shame and unsupported indispensability. Preserve the viewer’s choice; required material qualifications remain. |
| D7 | Understandable causal mechanism: quote old cause and what the intervention changes so a cold viewer can explain cause → action → result in ordinary words. “It does it for you” and a disconnected feature list fail. Missing explanation REVISE; unverified behavior BLOCKED. No forced internals/tutorial; an inline roster can carry a real mechanism. |

## G21 reference-transfer stress test

1. **Closest analogue:** search audience/problem/mechanism as well as topic/brand. Inspect even a weak close match. Record actual device and similarities/differences; a shared money hook is insufficient.
2. **Weak or repeated control:** examine available underperforming neighbours or reruns beyond top-ranked winners. Preserve observed date/window, denominator and sponsorship uncertainty. Missing metrics are unknown, not zero. Different views on repeated copy do not prove why performance changed.
3. **Recent coverage:** inspect authorized recent published AND approved-unpublished work. Record search terms/scope, dates/IDs and meaningful task/mechanism/proof overlap. A new product name is not freshness; a useful new demonstration or revisit may be.
4. **Transfer decision:** explain actual engine retained, what cannot transfer, new value and uncertainty. Reasonable unsuccessful control search means “no control found”, not an invented failure or automatic veto. Never-attempted search and missing necessary parent evidence cannot pass.

## Result discipline

Show PASS/REVISE/BLOCKED/N/A with reason and exact evidence, not “checked”. Keep writing quality, evidence completeness, version-specific approval, resource delivery, actual human performance and final production clearance distinct. Fix local failures before treating evidence as an external blocker. BLOCKED includes attempted lookup, missing item, owner and clearing action. Every changed sentence invalidates the prior full review; inherited evidence must resolve against the latest exact copy.
