# Writing, research and handoff workflow

## Assignment and voice

Establish audience and its real knowledge level, goal, platform, duration, organic/sponsored context, actual offer, deadline, CTA/fulfillment, authorized sources, voice samples, locked words and approval status. Ask only for missing information that changes the result; label reasonable assumptions. Do not infer a seller's location, legal jurisdiction, audience metrics or credentials.

When duration is unspecified, useful short-form starting bands are a quick hit at 15–25 seconds, organic explanation at 35–45, sponsor/walkthrough at 40–60. These are editable production defaults, not measured guarantees. A human pace and platform context override them. Plan no more than two unresolved major prediction loops in a short reel; no compulsory twist if a useful roster is a better engine.

Read two or three authorized approved/voice samples when available. Missing samples do not mean inventing an author's voice or stealing somebody else's script. Approved wording is evidence of taste, not measured audience performance. Choose a working hypothesis openly when examples are absent.

## Truth sheet before angle

| Bucket | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Locked facts | Names, capabilities, eligibility, price, dates, limits, exact URL/CTA and disclosure requirements; preserve factual meaning and verbatim locks. |
| Translatable requirements | Convert what must be communicated into this audience's ordinary outcome/task language. |
| Optional marketing copy | Keep only language that actually adds value; no mandatory adjectives or sponsor-provided feature recital. |
| Unsupported/risky claims | Verify or cut. Missing evidence stays visible; plausible is not substantiated. |

Build claim rows for qualitative claims as well as numbers: exact claim, intended spoken wording, source URL or real supplied artifact, checked date, evidence type, conditions, verification result. Primary documentation proves documented scope; it does not prove you ran the product. User claims stay attributed until independently checked. Inferences must be labelled as such and cannot sound like observed tests.

For current facts, retrieve actual current primary evidence where tools permit. A sponsor brief alone is not independent evidence. Do not run paid or externally mutating tests without authorization. Regulated claims, health/fitness promises, finance, vehicle financing and safety claims require suitable authoritative evidence and applicable disclosure review; no health results, rates, approval or safety guarantees from thin samples. A jurisdiction-specific campaign may need qualified human review. This skill does not provide legal/medical clearance.

Survey the complete relevant capabilities/surfaces, then select what the audience most wants. Record important jobs included, combined or omitted. A collective term must not turn a few verified features into universal coverage. A sourced large capacity can exceed the filmed sample; distinguish long-run potential from instant output, guaranteed quality and personal results.

For sponsored work, verify actual brief obligations and required disclosures, not just the sponsor's preferred adjectives. If authorized current feed evidence is available, assess recent organic/sponsored context, time since related content, repeated mechanism/hook/CTA and saturation, with specific observations and low/medium/high judgment. Otherwise say cadence not assessed. This is advisory, not a ratio/quota, a creative gate waiver, permission to conceal sponsorship or authorization to post. A repeat campaign needs real distinct usefulness, not merely a new feature name. Use the user's actual timezone/platform guidance only when supplied or verified; no borrowed posting window.

## Reference library: own inputs, no private dependency

Use only user-authorized files/exports or public examples whose facts you can inspect. There is no prescribed database, paid tool, private workspace or external model requirement.

Where available inspect 5–10 actual candidate transcripts, not captions alone. Record IDs/links, publication dates, sponsorship labels/uncertainty, observation times, denominator, metric and window. For share-oriented reels, shares/views at a consistent first-next-day age is more useful than incomparable lifetime totals. A configurable 24–30-hour window is one defensible collection convention, not exact 24-hour evidence; disclose actual ages. For conversions/education choose the brief's relevant metric and comparable window without claiming a different metric proves shares or retention. Do not pad a small cohort or treat missing metrics as zero.

Select a primary structural parent and normally two to four supporting examples where available. Describe the actual emotional/causal device: an expanding useful job roster, repeated deepening fear with earned relief, a status contrast, an evidence-led role change. Do not reduce all parents to money hooks or require every script to use the same arc.

Stress-test closest audience/problem/mechanism analogue; available weak/repeated control beyond winners; recent published AND approved-unpublished coverage; then transfer decision. Preserve transcript quotes/observation windows and avoid causal claims from correlation. A weak control challenges your story but is not an automatic veto. An unsuccessful permitted search can honestly find no control. Missing measured parent data cannot pass G21/G28.

**Cold start:** write a provisional script from verified claims and reasoned creative hypotheses; run every other applicable check and mark G21/G28 BLOCKED, “winner transfer unverified”. State what data would clear the block. Users can use the draft and explicitly approve wording without having a measured history, but neither approval nor usefulness turns those two gates green. No private library ships with this package.

## Concept lock and audience breadth

Record sender/recipient and shared pain; legitimate content territory; CORE/ADJACENT/PERIPHERAL fit and prerequisites; chosen reference device and uncertainty; supported upward reveal or reasoned N/A; meaningful useful change; real demo/artifact. Specific meaning is not necessarily a specific business model. Start from the widest truthful shared pain the brief wants; illustrate later. If a product inherently serves a narrow task, declare the mismatch rather than imply all-purpose functionality.

Write the causal spine: current burden AND cause → source-supported intervention changing the cause → real task/actor → viewer's changed role → earned conclusion. Then make it audible in narration, not merely a persuasive note below it. Assess D1–D7. A superficial convenience is not a replaced service. A gym participant's changed role need not be “agency approver”; use their actual decisions and capability.

Map topic-specific audience states: never done, just started, outsourced, struggling, competent. Four STAY in the default hook scan, beginners mandatory. A single concrete outcome can serve several body states. Do not invent an outsourcing scenario, add five persona lines or mistake experience coverage for account-wide breadth. Purposeful expert/brief restrictions are explicit customizations, not broad-appeal passes.

## Hook and growing body

Write opening and line two separately from body-polishing. Target a short comprehensible claim carrying H1 benefit lever, H2 emotion, H3 real unanswered question and H4 relevant credibility. Credibility normally uses a recognizable relevant product/institution/credential; public adaptation also accepts directly observable evidence rather than forcing an irrelevant famous name. Record this route and verify it. Do not imply endorsement or insert celebrity names for recognition alone.

Strong source-supported emotion stays. Scope the hook truthfully in its own words; line two makes it bigger, more concrete or more evidenced, not less true. Reject vague suspense, bare unknown names, unidentified pronouns and numbers without a counted object. A viewer should restate the premise immediately.

Build a sentence ladder, not a beat-only summary. Each sentence adds meaningful value or rising consequence, proof, control or an actionable next step. Roster rungs must expand the real outcome, not decompose the ceiling into smaller features. Repeated fear must deepen in actual consequence/evidence, not repeat generic warnings. Every seam must carry value and clear referents. No one-sentence-per-gate, axis quota, forced threat, automatic head fake or canned same-setup ending.

Preview an actual result by the early lines where feasible; thread proof through the build. Specify starting state, exact action, visible result and consequence. A simple screen plan can support a real filmable artifact but cannot create a run that never happened. Label sample/illustration/documentation honestly. Hold the reusable artifact long enough to read/screenshot in the planned production; inspect actual holds after editing.

Resolve material promise-raised doubts with verified capability/control/proof preferably inline. No all-objections quota or redundant reassurance. Keep verified secondary setup/pricing in the resource if the spoken claim is already accurately scoped. Never omit a material condition to manufacture a better hook; narrow or cut that claim instead. Sponsor disclosure is not optional because it interrupts pacing.

Finish with the specified resource/next step and recipient-specific share invitation when applicable. Do not silently replace a booking campaign with comment bait. G7's saveable value is independent of CTA collection, and a resource must actually exist before you claim it does.

## Spoken edit, full review and completion

Use accurate collective language instead of three-item recitals; preserve supported coverage. Natural connectors replace punctuation-only transitions and fragment runs. A deliberate repeated opener can be an escalating engine, not an automatic Humanizer failure. Translate audience-unfamiliar terms and quantities to felt meaning using sourced arithmetic. Run all 33 patterns, factual/locked parity, actual labelled rehearsal, then G26/G30 from the viewer's seat.

Print complete G1–G30, H1–H4, D1–D7 and 33 Humanizer results, sentence ladder, audience states, truth sheet and production notes. Give every applicable result its own evidence. Separate generated mechanical findings from judgment and independently obtained evidence. An independent cold reviewer should judge G17/G18/G26 and first-listen clarity from the script itself, not the author's persuasion notes, when available; label an author-only review honestly. No pretending one author and an automated scan are independent reviewers.

Keep latest version on top and real superseded copies below dated reason/status dividers. Retain genuine rejection quotes and approvals with scope/version/hash; superseding an approved copy is not retroactive rejection. Every edit requires a full fresh review and current receipt checks. An approval quotation stored in a packet cannot authenticate itself; the active reviewer must confirm the actual user message before recording or sending.

Maintain an authorized local reference/library record of actual approvals; idempotent version/hash prevents duplicate or conflicting records. Later attach real published identifiers/observations only after matching the exact content. Never promote approved-unmeasured work to winner status, silently backfill a private store, repair collectors or create background jobs.

Final production checks the actual assembled reel at phone scale, real crop/subtitles/selected voice/platform overlay, full normal-speed audiovisual playback and timestamped readable proof. Note rights, source/product testing, human take, safe zones, resource availability and CTA delivery separately. Hash/version identifies the actual reviewed cut; edited bytes require re-review. No isolated asset or synthetic read proves final-cut comprehension. No pass or wording approval authorizes publishing, scheduling, uploading, messages, purchases or account changes.

When the user explicitly requests an external handoff, confirm the exact approved version, destination and current action scope, exclude private review/analytics from the spoken body, perform only that authorized action and read the destination back before claiming delivery. An external write failure stays undelivered, never a successful handoff because a local packet exists. Retry the same version/event identity rather than duplicate records; changed content is a new approval question. This skill supplies the content/evidence contract, not a specific account connector or permission to install one.
